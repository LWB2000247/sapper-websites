1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Sapper Advanced Technologies LLC, doing business as Sapper Websites ("Sapper," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit sapperwebsites.com (the "Site"), submit our contact form, or communicate with us.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us through our contact form:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number (optional);
  • your business name;
  • a description of what you need;
  • your current website or domain (optional);
  • your consent confirmation (the checkbox on the form).

Information from our communications and agreements:

  • the content of emails and messages you send us and our replies;
  • if you become a client, records of the web build agreement we send you and your confirmation of it, such as the date and time of confirmation, the email address used, and the version of the agreement accepted;
  • if you make a payment, limited payment information we receive from our third-party payment processor, such as your name, email address, amount, date, and payment status. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

Information collected automatically:

  • Hosting logs: Our hosting provider may automatically log technical information when you visit the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and operation of the service.
  • Microsoft Clarity: We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the Site and to improve it. Clarity uses cookies and similar technologies to collect usage data, such as pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, mouse movements, device and browser information, and approximate location, and creates anonymized session replays of visits. We configure Clarity so that text you type into form fields is masked and is not captured in session replays. Microsoft Clarity loads only after you click Accept in our cookie banner. If you decline or make no choice, Clarity does not run and sets no cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the 'Cookie settings' link in the footer.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your request and communicate with you about it;
  • prepare and send you a web build agreement, confirmation link, and payment link if we move forward together;
  • keep records of agreement confirmations;
  • design, build, and deliver your website and provide related support;
  • process payments and keep business, tax, and accounting records;
  • operate, secure, analyze, and improve the Site;
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

4. Text Messages

We send text messages (SMS) only to people who have given separate, express consent to receive them. Providing a phone number on our form is not consent to receive text messages. If you consent to text messages, you can opt out at any time by replying STOP. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or text messaging consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share personal information only as follows:

  • FormSubmit: Our contact form is sent through the third-party form service FormSubmit, which delivers your form submission to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages: The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., which processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the Site.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Usage data, cookies, and session replays are processed by Microsoft as described above. Microsoft's handling of this data is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Email provider: Our email provider stores and transmits the emails we send and receive, including form submissions.
  • Payment processor: When applicable, our third-party payment processor processes payments for accepted agreements.
  • Project platforms: When needed to build or launch your website and at your direction, we may share information with third parties such as domain registrars or hosting companies.
  • Legal reasons: We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, property, or safety or that of others.
  • Business transfers: If our business is sold, merged, or reorganized, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

These service providers process information to provide their services to us and may also process it as described in their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Your Choices

Microsoft Clarity loads only after you click Accept in our cookie banner. If you decline or make no choice, Clarity does not run and sets no cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the 'Cookie settings' link in the footer. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can use browser extensions or settings that block analytics scripts. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from using the Site or submitting the contact form. The Site does not currently respond to Do Not Track browser signals.

We keep inquiries and related communications for as long as needed for our business relationship with you and to meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. If you contact us and decide not to work with us, we will delete your inquiry on request. Records we are required to keep by law, such as agreement and payment records, are kept for as long as those obligations require. Microsoft Clarity and our other service providers keep data according to their own retention periods.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on the Site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or other sensitive information through our form or by email.
